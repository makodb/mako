## Observations To Carry Into The Thesis

- The no-ReplayPool Single-Raft run reports high leader-side throughput at
  `t=11` (`593k ops/s`), but follower replay progress is only about
  `143k ops/s`. This should not be presented as a fully healthy replicated
  execution path.
- With `1:1` ReplayPool, Single-Raft reports about `526k ops/s` at `t=11` and
  follower replay progress is about `499k ops/s`, which is much closer to the
  leader-side throughput.
- Multi-Raft and Paxos also keep follower replay progress close to leader-side
  throughput at `t=11` (`484k` vs `510k` for Multi-Raft, `450k` vs `472k` for
  Paxos).
- The full ReplayPool matrix confirms that leader-side throughput alone is not
  enough to evaluate Single-Raft. At `t=11`, zero replay threads keep only
  about `23.5%` of the leader's committed history replayed on the follower,
  while eight and eleven replay threads stay near `95%` completeness. Four
  replay threads are adequate at moderate thread counts but fall to about
  `81.5%` completeness at `t=11`.
- Cloud-SSD throughput flattens near `340k ops/s` for all three backends at high
  thread counts. At `t=11`, all three write about `7.49 GB`, about `24.6k`
  FakeDisk writes, and about `733 bytes` per committed transaction. The body
  figure uses bytes per transaction instead of max wait because max wait is a
  worst-case statistic and is expected to be noisy.
- The three-run no-disk `t=11` reruns are stable: CV is `0.06%` for
  Single-Raft, `0.34%` for Multi-Raft, and `0.57%` for Paxos.
