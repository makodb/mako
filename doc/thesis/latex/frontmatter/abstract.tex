\noindent
Mako is a high-throughput, geo-replicated transactional key-value store whose
original replication backend used Paxos. Mako keeps transactions fast by
separating leader-side execution, replication of transaction log records, and
follower-side replay into local database state. This thesis asks whether that
execute-replicate-replay pipeline can be preserved when Paxos is replaced with
Raft.

The answer depends on systems integration, not only on the consensus protocol.
Raft can order commands correctly, but Mako also needs the right replication
topology, leader placement, and replay parallelism. This thesis therefore
implements and evaluates two Raft designs. Multi-Raft is the direct,
Paxos-shaped replacement: separate worker and replication lanes use separate
Raft groups. Single-Raft is the consolidated design: all lanes share one Raft
log, with partition information carried inside each command.

Two mechanisms make the Raft integration fit Mako. Preferred leader election
aligns Raft leadership with the process where Mako submits and first executes
transactions, while preserving ordinary Raft failover and safety rules.
ReplayPool makes Single-Raft practical by keeping Raft apply lightweight and
moving expensive follower replay into parallel replay workers.

The evaluation shows that Raft remains in Paxos's performance class when these
mechanisms preserve Mako's pipeline. In the in-memory 11-worker experiment,
Single-Raft with ReplayPool reaches about 526K committed transactions/s, while
Multi-Raft reaches about 510K and the original Paxos backend reaches about
472K. Without ReplayPool, Single-Raft reports high leader-side throughput but
followers replay far fewer records, showing that leader throughput alone is
misleading. Under a simulated Cloud-SSD persistence model, all three backends
flatten near 340K transactions/s while reporting comparable modeled storage
work. Overall, Raft can replace Mako's Paxos backend when topology, preferred
leadership, and replay are engineered around Mako's original pipeline.
