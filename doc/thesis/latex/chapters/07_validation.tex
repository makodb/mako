\chapter{Correctness and Validation}

The validation chapter answers why the reader should trust the performance
results.

It should not become a test manual. The body needs a compact validation matrix
that connects test evidence to the thesis claims.

The most important validation rule is metric discipline. The thesis uses
\code{throughput\_ops\_sec} as the leader-side benchmark rate. The \code{replay\_batch\_p1}
and \code{replay\_batch\_p2} counters are follower-progress sanity counters. They
are essential for interpreting Single-Raft, but they are not the headline
metric.
