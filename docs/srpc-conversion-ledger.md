## Ordering notes
Ordering respects the ledger dep graph bottom-up; only files with motion lift-dsl / rewrite-std / kernel-libc / kernel-asm are sliced. Excluded: [subsumed-by-wire] (basetypes, serializable, frame_codec, internal_protocol — already the M1 wire layer), [dead] (incl. completion_tracker + idempotency), [generated], and [stays-cpp-test] (those remain the C++ golden harness; each slice lands with new Rust unit tests PLUS the matching stays-cpp-test suites re-run against the transpiled C++ as acceptance). Slices 2 and 3 are mutually independent — they can land in either order or in parallel; everything else is a strict chain. The fiber runtime (slice 4) and epoll transport (slice 5) sit exactly where their first consumers force them: fiber_channel/tcp_channel (slice 6) are the first files needing both. Cross-cutting decisions to settle once, in slice 1: (a) zero-dep tension — mmap/mprotect stacks, socket syscalls, errno constants (EAGAIN/ETIMEDOUT for request_queue) need either one gated libc dep or raw-syscall kernels; recommend a tiny `sys` module isolating this, mirroring the kernel-libc classification; (b) panic-vs-Result policy (future.cpp logic_error, callbacks catch(...) swallow -> catch_unwind). Two deliberate placements: reactor.cpp#stackless-tasks moved to slice 5 (it is executor-slot machinery on Reactor, but its Rust shape — std Future + Waker on the poll loop — co-designs with poll-thread, and nothing in slice 4 consumes it); reactor.cpp#quorum-event deferred to slice 7 because it lives in namespace janus with ~41 deptran call sites — it is consumer-facing surface, not rrr-internal, and landing it earlier forces deptran churn before the crate core is stable. Similarly, Reactor::clients_/dangling_ips_ (deptran connection registry parked on reactor-core) should be hoisted into a C++-side bridge during slice 4 rather than ported into the crate core, per the ledger's own note. Known-hazard carryovers flagged per slice: clang22 hashbrown crash family applies to transpiled HashMap/HashSet code (any_message, poll-thread, client/server) — keep the existing workarounds in the generated C++; wire-visible constants (RpcError i32 ranges, errno numerics) must stay bit-exact since the ported wire layer transports them.
